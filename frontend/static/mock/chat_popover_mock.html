JPMC Customer Assistant — Popover Scroll & Mailbox Slot Reveal

The design vision: The popover card sits directly above the bottom chat prompt bar, aligned with 4px margins on each side. When summoned or clicked, it rolls upward like an authoritative parchment scroll or rises like a letter emerging from a mailbox slot. When minimized (_) or closed (✕), it rolls smoothly back down into the console bar slot without obstructing the desk grids behind it.
