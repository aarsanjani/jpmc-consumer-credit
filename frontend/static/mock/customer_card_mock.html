Where it sits

Replace the two static case cards in "The case" column (#caseCard1, #caseCard2, index.html ~L99-123) with this one card. Those cards already describe DSP00033 (debit) and DSP00034 (credit) for CUST00001, so the toggle maps one-to-one onto what's there today. It follows the mast #customerSelect chip.

Backend: a new read-only endpoint (e.g. GET /api/customer/cards?customer_id= in backend/ui_insights.py) that calls golden 01, then golden 07 → golden 08 per dispute → golden 10 per card.

Gaps found while building this

  • No golden query lists all of a customer's cards. Golden 07 only returns cards that have an open dispute, so a card with no dispute won't appear.
  • Golden 07's default open statuses include PROVISIONAL_ISSUED, but the data says PROVISIONAL_CREDIT_ISSUED. That hides DSP00033, so CUST00001's debit card (CARD00004) is missed by golden 07. I reached it through golden 08 on DSP00033.
  • There's no account-number column in accounts, only account_id, so "last 4 of account number" is n/r.
  • Golden 01 says CUST00001 has 1 active card and 1 active token, but both cards we can see are FROZEN and all 4 tokens are REVOKED. The count likely covers a card golden 07 can't see.
